Brainstorm the intervention · artifact harness

Eleven places to make agents leave pages that teach

“Here's the problem: agents in this harness finish tasks without leaving anything a person can use to understand the work, and template-driven pages had the right form but taught nothing. Search the codebase and brainstorm the places we could intervene, from cheapest to most ambitious. I'll tell you which ones resonate.”

The pattern in this branch's history: every mechanism that checks form got satisfied by form. The template era generated pages from typed records and registered them in a hashed ledger; they still taught nothing. What found real defects in the trial was a second model reading the page against the code. The gate only makes sure there is a page to read.

The blindspot pass established seven holes in the gate; they are cited below as B01–B07 rather than shown again.

#8

An independent reviewer per page

in this PR, as a briefL
docs/skills/review-artifact.md · docs/ARTIFACTS.md step 5

A fresh agent checks three claims against the code, uses every interaction and returns PASS or REVISE, with at most two rewrites. In the trial its reviews of the notes and quiz found two product bugs: save overwrote a newer board over 256 KiB, and the writer could pick a format older than the build reads. The cost driver is eight independently reviewed pages plus two rewrites (reference, quiz). Git shows 44 minutes from the discovery commit to the completion record, not counting the discovery pages written before it.

StopsB and E (the trial's evidence). D only if a review runs; no code requires one.

AlternativeWithout it, nothing in the stack distinguishes the trial's pages from the template era's.
