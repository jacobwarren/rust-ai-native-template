Buy-in · brief-calibration

The page reviewer now checks what it used to leave to chance

Every review now runs four fixed checks, and the author brief has a matching rule under each. In the rerun, every defect the old brief missed was raised in every run. The cost: two committed pages that used to pass now fail, mostly for real defects.

“Package the plan, the implementation notes and the evidence into a single doc I can share to get buy-in on shipping the calibrated author and reviewer briefs. Lead with the demo.”

Ask: confirm or change six calls (section 05). None changes the shape of the work. After that come the quiz page, the record's review answers and the merge.

02The pitch

With the old brief, 21 fresh reviews of six known pages caught a page running long every time. A page that disagreed with its own evidence was caught only one to two times in three, because that was left to whichever claims a reviewer happened to sample. Now every review first runs consistent, provenance, cited and ticket. The author brief has a rule under each, and a test keeps the two in step.
