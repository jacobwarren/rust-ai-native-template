Choice ①Two widened checks, or the ticket's words
Plan's pick: consistent and provenance (interview Q3)

The two checks the calibration missed are cases of two classes. Named by class, they also catch three failures in next-prompt's log that the calibration never sampled: the implementation draft's 5 / 7 tile against its own "In progress" entry, unbuilt --notes - behaviour described as existing, and a quiz labelled "Real output" over hand-typed strings (blindspots 08).

Cost: the ticket's first acceptance bullet changes its wording (A4).

Alternative: reply, captured, cited, ticket

Matches the acceptance bullet word for word. The reply check owes nothing on implementation, buy-in and quiz pages, so its n/a becomes "not a discovery page" (review SKILL.md:59–60).

Pick this if you want the test to pin the ticket's words. The three logged cases are recorded in EVALUATION.md as known gaps.

Choice ②What counts as a number's source
Plan's pick: a source on the page, or a command named there (Q5)

A reader can follow a named command by running it. This fails the buy-in draft's 9 / 9 CI checks pass at the branch head on a page that never names check ci (buy-in.round1.html:213). The committed buy-in page passes through the sentence under its tiles: "The nine checks in cargo xtask check ci, all passed" (buy-in.html:214).

Alternative: a source beside each tile

Stricter and simpler to judge. It fails all four tiles on the committed buy-in page (buy-in.html:209–212), which passed every fresh run, so that page's expected verdict turns to REVISE.

Pick this if tiles are read alone, for example in a screenshot.
