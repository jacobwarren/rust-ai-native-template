Choose the recovery investment

Three credible responses to ambiguous save results. Compare the behavior you buy, not just implementation effort.

Why start with explain + inspect?

The driver already distinguishes storage_io from save_uncertain. Explaining that distinction can improve recovery without claiming a new durability guarantee. A recovery assistant is a separate investment, not a prerequisite to the honest message.

Doing nothing: the CLI retains its correct error, but a new UI could still collapse it into a misleading generic failure.
