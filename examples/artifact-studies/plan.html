Decision most likely to change · user promise

Expose the commit boundary, or collapse every failure?

Recommendation: expose it. The contract already returns different errors; a generic toast discards the most useful recovery information. This proposal adds presentation, not a stronger storage guarantee.

The fault selector is an explanatory model. The public driver has no fault-injection switch.
